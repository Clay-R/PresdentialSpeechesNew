ANALYSIS:
Earlier speeches were shorter, averaging about 4,037 words compared with 9,935 words for modern speeches.
Earlier speeches averaged 4–5 letters per word, while modern speeches averaged 5, so earlier words were not longer.
All target words appeared in every speech except united, which was absent from Roosevelt’s 1906 speech.
Our had the highest percentage in both eras, except in Roosevelt’s 1903 speech, where united led.
Vocabulary differences may reflect changing topics and priorities, while our helps create a sense of shared identity.
I found it interesting that modern speeches were much longer without having noticeably longer words.
